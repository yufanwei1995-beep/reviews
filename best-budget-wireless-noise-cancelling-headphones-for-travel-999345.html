Best for: Commuters who want thumping bass and long battery life.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can budget noise-cancelling headphones block airplane engine noise?

Yes, but not as effectively as premium models. The Soundcore Space One does an excellent job reducing engine drone, while others like the JBL Tune 770NC are better for bus or train noise. For a truly silent flight, you’d need to spend $300+, but these are close enough for most travelers.

Do I need an app for these headphones?

Not necessarily. The Soundcore Life Q30 and Space One offer app-based EQ and ANC modes, which can improve your experience. The Sony WH-CH720N has an app, but it’s not essential. The JBL and P30i don’t require an app at all.

Are these headphones comfortable for long flights?

Generally, yes. The Sony WH-CH720N and Soundcore Space One are both lightweight and have plush ear cups. The Anker P30i may feel cramped for larger ears after a few hours. I recommend trying them on before a long trip.

What about call quality?

Call quality varies. The Soundcore Space One and Sony WH-CH720N have decent microphones for calls in quiet environments. The JBL Tune 770NC and Soundcore Life Q30 are average—fine for quick calls, but not great in windy conditions.

Can I use these wireless headphones wired on a plane?

Yes, all of these models include a 3.5mm aux cable and a USB-C charging cable. You can use them wired even with the battery dead, which is essential for flights that still require a wired connection for in-flight entertainment.
