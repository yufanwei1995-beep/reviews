You're comparing two of the most popular "clean" whey proteins on the market. Both brands have cult followings. Both avoid artificial junk. Both cost more than the generic stuff at Walmart. But they are not the same, and one is clearly better for certain people.

This review is for anyone who wants a protein powder without artificial sweeteners, fillers, or proprietary blends. You want to know exactly what's in your scoop, and you're willing to pay a little extra for that transparency. Let's break down the real differences.

Quick Summary:

  • Who this is for: Lifters and athletes who prioritize ingredient quality over price and want maximum label transparency.
  • Top Pick: Transparent Labs Grass-Fed Whey Protein Isolate — cleaner protein source, slightly better flavor options, and zero artificial anything.
  • Price Range: Both cost between $1.50 and $2.20 per serving depending on size and sales. Legion is usually a bit cheaper per serving.

What to Look For in a Premium Whey Protein

Before we dive into the head-to-head, you need to know what separates a good protein from a great one. Here's the short list:

  • Protein Source: Isolate vs concentrate matters. Isolate has less lactose and fat, but costs more. Concentrate has more beneficial fats and peptides but can cause bloating in sensitive people.
  • Sweeteners: This is the big one. Transparent Labs uses stevia. Legion uses a mix of stevia and monk fruit. Both avoid sucralose and aspartame. But some people taste stevia's bitter aftertaste more than others.
  • Third-Party Testing: Both brands test every batch. Legion uses Informed Choice. Transparent Labs uses independent labs and publishes results. Neither is just paying for a sticker; they actually test.
  • Flavor Quality: A clean protein can taste like cardboard. A good one tastes like a dessert. We tested all the popular flavors.
  • Mixability: If it clumps, you'll hate it. Both use instantized ingredients, but results vary by flavor.
Pro tip: Check the "other ingredients" list. If you see "natural flavors" without specifics, ask the brand what that means. Transparent Labs lists every ingredient including the specific fruit extracts used for flavoring. Legion does too, which is rare.

Transparent Labs Grass-Fed Whey Protein Isolate

This is the flagship whey from Transparent Labs. It's 100% grass-fed whey protein isolate. That means almost no lactose and very little fat. Each serving has 28 grams of protein, 0 grams of sugar, and only 1 gram of carbs.

The label is ridiculously detailed. They list the specific amounts of every amino acid. You won't find a proprietary blend anywhere. The "natural flavors" aren't hidden either — they name the actual fruit extracts used. That's the level of transparency the brand is named for.

Flavors we tested:

  • Milk Chocolate: This is the crowd favorite. It tastes like a melted chocolate bar, not a chalky shake. Mixes well with water, but better with milk.
  • Vanilla: Solid but unspectacular. Slightly sweet, no weird aftertaste. Good for baking or adding to coffee.
  • Strawberry: Tastes like strawberry milk, not artificial candy. This surprised us.

The tradeoff: It's expensive. A 30-serving tub runs around $60. That's $2 per scoop. You can find cheaper isolates, but few with this level of ingredient quality. Also, the stevia aftertaste is noticeable in some flavors, especially the fruity ones. Vanilla and chocolate are safest.

Mixability: It mixes fine in a shaker bottle with 20-30 seconds of shaking. No blender needed. Some flavors clump slightly if you add liquid too fast.

Legion Athletics Whey+

Legion's whey is a blend of isolate and concentrate. That's actually a smart move — you get the fast-absorbing isolate plus the beneficial fats and peptides from concentrate. Each serving has 22 grams of protein, 5.5 grams of BCAAs, and very little carbs.

Legion uses both stevia and monk fruit for sweetness. That combo helps mask stevia's bitter edge. The result is a sweeter, more dessert-like taste compared to Transparent Labs. If you have a sweet tooth, you'll prefer Legion.

Flavors we tested:

  • Chocolate Peanut Butter: The best flavor either brand makes. Tastes like a Reese's cup in shake form. Dangerously easy to drink.
  • Dutch Chocolate: Good, but not as rich as Transparent Labs' chocolate. Slightly sweeter.
  • Vanilla: Clean and light. Mixes well with oatmeal and pancakes.
  • Fruit Cereal: Weird. Tastes like Froot Loops milk, which some people love. We found it too artificial-tasting despite using natural sweeteners.

The tradeoff: You get less protein per scoop (22g vs 28g). That means you need more powder per serving to hit your protein targets, which eats into the cost savings. Also, the concentrate can cause bloating in lactose-sensitive people, even though it's a small amount.

Mixability: Excellent. Legion's instantized formula mixes almost instantly in cold water. No clumps, no foam. This is the best-mixing protein we've tested.

Other Options Worth Considering

If neither of these fits your needs, here are two solid alternatives:

Levels Grass Fed Whey Protein

Similar to Transparent Labs but cheaper. Uses grass-fed isolate, stevia sweetening, and has 25g protein per scoop. The chocolate flavor is decent, but the vanilla is flat. Good budget option if Transparent Labs is too pricey.

Promix Whey Protein

Cold-processed, grass-fed, and unflavored options available. If you hate sweeteners entirely, Promix's unflavored whey is the cleanest thing on the market. But it tastes like milk powder — you'll need to blend it with fruit or cocoa.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which has more protein per scoop?

Transparent Labs wins with 28g vs Legion's 22g. That's a 6g difference, which matters if you're tracking macros tightly.

Which tastes better?

Legion wins overall, especially with Chocolate Peanut Butter. But Transparent Labs' Milk Chocolate is better than any Legion chocolate flavor. It depends on what you're looking for.

Are these good for lactose-intolerant people?

Transparent Labs is the safer choice since it's 100% isolate. Legion has some concentrate, which contains a tiny bit more lactose. If you're very sensitive, go with Transparent Labs.

Can I use these for baking or cooking?

Yes. Both work in protein pancakes, oats, and smoothies. Transparent Labs' vanilla is better for baking because it's less sweet. Legion's flavors are more dessert-like and can overpower recipes.

Which is better for weight loss?

Transparent Labs. Fewer carbs (1g vs 3g) and fewer calories per scoop. But the difference is small, so pick based on taste and budget first.

Bottom Line Verdict

If you want the absolute cleanest protein with the most protein per scoop, buy Transparent Labs Grass-Fed Whey Isolate. It's more expensive, but you're paying for 100% isolate, full label transparency, and zero lactose concerns. Stick with Milk Chocolate or Vanilla flavors. If you have a sweet tooth and want the best-tasting protein on the market, buy Legion Whey+ in Chocolate Peanut Butter. You get slightly less protein per scoop, but the taste and mixability are unmatched. Either way, you're getting a product that's third-party tested and free of artificial junk. You can't go wrong — just pick based on your priorities.
